A 360 booth for Naperville celebrations

Naperville hosts plenty of weddings, sweet 16s, graduation parties and company events. A 360 booth works at all of them. Guests step on the platform, the camera spins, and they scan a QR code to save a slow-motion video with your custom overlay.

Grad parties and sweet 16s

Backyard graduation parties and sweet 16s are a perfect fit. We set up on any flat 10×10 ft space near an outlet, the attendant handles the line, and every guest gets a video with the guest of honor's name on it. Signature (2 hours, $440) covers most home parties.

Weddings and the western suburbs

For weddings, Premiere (4 hours, $880) adds cold sparks, pro lighting, stanchions and a carpet. Elite (3 hours, $660) is a great middle option. We also serve Aurora, Lisle, Wheaton and Bolingbrook. Depending on your venue, a small travel fee may apply, and we'll include it in your quote up front.

FAQ

Naperville 360 Booth Questions

Do you travel to Naperville?

Yes. Send us your venue address and we will include any travel fee in your quote up front.

Can you set up in a backyard?

Yes, on a flat area about 10×10 ft with a standard outlet nearby.

How much space does the 360 booth need?

About a 10×10 ft area with one standard power outlet nearby.

How many people fit on the platform?

Usually 1–4 at a time. Turns are unlimited, so groups can go again and again.

Is setup included in my hours?

No. We arrive early to set up and pack down after, so every hour you book is booth time.

How do guests get their videos?

They scan a QR code at the booth and the video goes straight to their phone.
