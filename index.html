CHICAGO 360 PHOTO BOOTH FAQ

How it works, space and sharing—answered.

How does a 360 photo booth work?+

Guests stand on the platform while a camera circles them. Our attendant guides the moment, then the finished slow-motion video is ready to share.

How long is each 360 video?+

Most sessions take about 60–90 seconds from stepping onto the platform to receiving the finished video.

How much space does the booth need?+

Plan for a clear 10 × 10-foot area with a standard power outlet nearby.

How many guests can use it per hour?+

Throughput depends on group size, but our attendant keeps the line moving and helps guests get ready before their turn.

Do guests get their videos immediately?+

Yes. Guests can receive and share their videos at the event when venue internet or cellular service is available.

What areas do you serve?+

We provide 360 photo booth rentals throughout Chicago and surrounding suburbs, including Skokie, Evanston and other Chicagoland communities.
