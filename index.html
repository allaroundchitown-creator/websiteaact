What’s included?

Every package includes unlimited 360 videos, a custom overlay, props, an attendant, instant sharing, setup and breakdown. Premiere adds a backdrop, spark machines, professional lighting, stanchions and carpet.

Space needed?

A clear 10 × 10 ft area with a standard outlet nearby. We’ll confirm access and setup with your venue.

Custom overlays?

Yes—add your names, date, event colors or branding. Design yours above, then save it to your booking request.

Travel area?

Chicago and Chicagoland. Include your city when booking so we can confirm any travel fees.

How do I reserve?

Submit your event details on our booking page. We’ll confirm availability and your selections; your deposit then secures the date.
