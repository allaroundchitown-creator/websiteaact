How much does a 360 photo booth cost in Chicago?

Our wedding packages are $800 for two hours and $1,200 for four hours. Every rental includes an attendant, unlimited videos, a custom overlay, props, instant sharing, setup and breakdown. Travel fees may apply; we confirm your final quote before booking.

How far ahead should we book our wedding booth?

Check availability once your wedding date and venue are set. Send your date and venue city through the form so we can confirm availability before you choose your package.

Are 360 photo booths good for wedding receptions?

They give guests an activity to enjoy together and a video keepsake. Plan the rental around cocktail hour or reception dancing, and coordinate the booth location with your venue so it stays clear of main walkways.

How much space does the booth need?

Plan for a clear 10-by-10-foot area with a standard outlet nearby. Tell us about your venue so we can confirm the setup and access.

How long should I book for a wedding?

Four hours works well for the busiest part of a reception. Two hours suits a shorter window, while four hours gives you extended coverage.

How do guests get their videos?

Our attendant helps guests receive and share their videos during the event. Instant delivery depends on reliable venue internet or cellular service.

Is an attendant included?

Yes. Every package includes an attendant to operate the booth, guide guests and help with video sharing.

How do deposits and reservations work?

We confirm your date, package and booking details first. Your date is secured after the required deposit is received. We’ll explain the payment terms before you reserve.

Do you charge for travel?

Travel fees may apply depending on your venue. Share your event city and we’ll include any travel costs in your quote.

Can you match our wedding colors?

Yes. Your custom video overlay can include your names, wedding date and colors to complement your reception.

Plan the booth around your reception

Cocktail hour gives guests an activity while everyone settles into the celebration.
