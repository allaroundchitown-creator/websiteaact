FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much space do you need?

About a 10×10 ft area with one standard power outlet nearby.

How many people fit on the platform?

Usually 1–4 at a time. Unlimited turns, so groups can go again and again.

Will someone run the booth?

Yes — an attendant is with the booth the whole time.

Is setup included in my hours?

No. We arrive early to set up and pack down after — your package hours are all booth time.

How do guests get their videos?

They scan a QR code at the booth and the video goes straight to their phone.

What areas do you serve?

Chicago and the surrounding suburbs. Further out? Ask us — a travel fee may apply.
