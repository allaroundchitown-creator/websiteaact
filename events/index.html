A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

The details,
taken care of.

What’s included?

Unlimited 360 videos during your booked time, a custom overlay, an attendant, props, instant sharing, setup and breakdown. Instant delivery depends on reliable internet or cellular service at the venue.

How much room do we need?

Plan for a clear 10-by-10-foot area with a standard outlet nearby. We’ll confirm venue access and setup arrangements before the event.

Can the videos match our event?

Your overlay can include names, an event date, colors or business branding. We confirm the design details with you before the celebration.

Do you travel to the suburbs?

Yes. We serve Chicago and Chicagoland, including Skokie, Evanston, Naperville, Schaumburg, Arlington Heights, Oak Brook and Des Plaines. Share your city so we can include any travel costs in your quote.

Does submitting the form reserve our date?

No. We confirm availability and your booking details first. Your date is secured after the required deposit is received under your confirmed booking terms.
