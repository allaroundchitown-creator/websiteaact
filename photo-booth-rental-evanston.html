A flexible fit for Evanston events

Evanston’s mix of lakefront celebrations, university gatherings, hotels and neighborhood venues calls for entertainment that can adapt to different timelines. Weddings, campus events and private parties

Couples use the booth as reception entertainment that brings both families together. Student groups and schools can add it to formal events, graduations and celebrations.
