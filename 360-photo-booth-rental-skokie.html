YOUR RECEPTION, YOUR WAY

A wedding celebration close to home.

For couples planning a Skokie wedding, the reception often brings together guests from Chicago and the North Shore. A 360 booth gives those groups an easy activity to enjoy together, without needing a separate outing or a complicated schedule. Place it within sight of the celebration so relatives and friends can join between dancing and conversation.

Skokie receptions can take place in banquet rooms, hotel event spaces or smaller private venues. The room layout matters more than the venue label: reserve a clear booth area before seating, catering stations and entertainment equipment are finalized. Ask your coordinator where guests will gather after dinner, then choose a booth position that feels part of the reception while leaving a safe route around it.

A LITTLE PLANNING GOES A LONG WAY

Your Skokie questions.

Can we include the booth in a smaller Skokie reception?

Yes, provided the venue can accommodate a clear 10-by-10-foot setup area and safe guest access. Send your venue city and date first; we can discuss the room layout before you reserve.

Will guests get their wedding videos during the event?

Yes, with reliable internet or cellular connectivity. Your attendant helps guests receive their clips. Check connectivity at the planned booth location with your venue, especially if the reception is in an interior room.

How do we reserve our wedding date?

Send your date and venue city through our current booking form. We confirm availability, discuss your rental length and provide pricing. Your date is secured after the deposit is received; submitting an inquiry alone does not reserve it.

Do you serve areas near Skokie?

We serve Skokie, Evanston, Lincolnwood, Morton Grove and Niles, Chicago and the surrounding suburbs. Share your actual venue city when you inquire so travel and setup details can be confirmed.
