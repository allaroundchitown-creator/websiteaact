BEFORE WE GET STARTED.

A few good
questions.

Are these one-time prices or monthly retainers?

Launch, Grow, and Dominate are one-time setup and build investments. Ongoing Meta Ads management, social media management, and continuing optimization are quoted separately. Monthly plans range from $500 to $1,500+ depending on the work included.

Is the advertising budget included?

No. Meta ad spend is separate from our setup and management fees. We’ll discuss your goals and budget before recommending a campaign plan.

Do you actually come out to photograph our booths?

Yes. Grow includes a Nikon Z7 content shoot, and Dominate includes a full content day with multiple booth and setup shoots. We confirm the location, travel arrangements, shot list, schedule, and deliverables in your proposal.

Can you work with our existing website and social accounts?

We’ll review what you already have and identify what can be kept, improved, or rebuilt. Share your website and social accounts when you inquire so we can recommend the right scope.

What does social media management include?

The setup packages establish your branding, content strategy, and the rollout listed in your selected package. Recurring posting, captions, new content, and continued management are defined in a separate ongoing plan.

Do you guarantee bookings every weekend?

No. The headline describes the goal we work toward. Booking results depend on your market, offer, pricing, ad budget, season, and how you respond to leads. We focus on building the content, website, campaigns, and follow-up process that support that goal.

How do we get started?

Tell us about your business below. We’ll review your current marketing, discuss the package that fits, and confirm deliverables, timeline, and payment terms in a proposal before work begins.
